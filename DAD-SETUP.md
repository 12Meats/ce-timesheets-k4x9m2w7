# CE Timesheets — Getting Started (5 minutes, one time)

The app link:

**https://12meats.github.io/ce-timesheets-k4x9m2w7/**

## Put it on your home screen (do this once)

1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** and go to the link above (Adrian can text it to you — just tap it).
2. Tap the **Share button** — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom middle of the screen.
3. Scroll down the list and tap **"Add to Home Screen."**
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. There's now a blue **CE** icon on your home screen, like any other app. Open it from there from now on — not from Safari.

## First time you open it

1. It asks you to **choose a 4-digit PIN**. Pick one you'll remember and enter it twice. (Tell Adrian what it is, just in case.)
2. Tap **"+ Add worker"** and add each of your guys. For each one you can also open their page and set:
   - Their **hourly rate** (so you can see estimated pay — ADP is still the official number).
   - Their **usual start time** (so the app can point out late days for you).

## Every week (entering the paper timesheets)

1. Open the app, enter your PIN.
2. Tap a worker → **"Enter hours."**
3. For each day, tap **Start**, type the time straight off the paper sheet (like `630`), tap **AM** or **PM** if it guessed wrong, tap **Done**. Same for **End**. No scroll wheels, ever.
4. **Lunch:** every full day automatically takes out 30 minutes for lunch. If someone worked through lunch that day, tap the little **"Lunch 30 min"** button on that day's row and it flips to "No lunch."
5. The hours math happens by itself — daily totals, the weekly total, and overtime past 40 hours.
6. Back on the workers screen, each name shows this week's hours and what they come to in dollars (once the worker has a rate on file). Same math as Payday: the rounded hours times the rate, overtime at time and a half.

## Payday

1. From the workers screen, tap **"Payday summary."**
2. Every worker is listed with two big numbers: **Reg** and **OT**. Those are exactly what you type into ADP. Done.

## Once in a while (important)

Tap the **gear** (Settings) → **"Send Data"** → text it to Adrian.

That's your backup. Everything lives on your phone, so if the phone is ever lost or replaced, that file is how we get everything back (Settings → Import Data). The app will gently remind you if it's been more than a month.
